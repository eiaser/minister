Fakir Mahbub Anam Swapan is a prominent Bangladeshi politician and businessman, currently serving as the Minister of Posts, Telecommunications and Information Technology, and Minister of Science and Technology.

Born in Tangail District, he follows in the footsteps of his father, a former MP. His political resilience led to a historic victory in the 13th General Election (Feb 2026), reclaiming the Tangail-1 seat for BNP after 45 years.

Known for his administrative vision, he played a key role in the formation of Dhanbari Upazila in 2006. Under the leadership of Prime Minister Tarique Rahman, he is now dedicated to transforming Bangladesh into a true technological powerhouse.
